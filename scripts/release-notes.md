# 0.4.10

The WASM edition is now named **sOU Default HUD**. Its package ID and sOU HUD identity remain unchanged.

The live HUD follows the reviewed cast layout: a compact central scoreboard, vertical series markers, map/pick strip above a larger Boltobserv radar, and mirrored blue/gold rosters.

Player cards place kill/death icon groups and cash outside, portraits in the middle, and a large carried weapon with HP inside. Utility and kit/bomb/armor use separate bottom groups. Health fill is confined to the loadout. Dead cards shorten and turn grey without health or inventory; the observed player has a white outline. Assists remain in accessible statistics.

Original CS2 SVGs, primary/pistol/knife priority, active observer equipment and the complete chronological killfeed are retained. All eight views and compatible panel settings remain available. No camera feed or private reference-media assets are included.

Import 0.4.10 and reopen outputs. The public SDK remains 0.2.0-preview.4. Browser verification does not replace native CS2/OBS acceptance.
