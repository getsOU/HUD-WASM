# CS2 agent portrait placeholders

Twenty transparent PNGs were downloaded unchanged from the [CSGOSKINS.GG agent catalogue](https://csgoskins.gg/categories/agent) on 2026-10-21 at the user's request. The site's [Counter-Terrorist list](https://csgoskins.gg/sides/counter-terrorists) and [Terrorist list](https://csgoskins.gg/sides/terrorists) establish the side assignments. `agent-portraits.json` records each character name, side, observer digit, item page, source image URL, dimensions, size and SHA-256. The identical inventory is included at `assets/agents/catalog.json`.

Counter-Strike character artwork and trademarks belong to Valve Corporation and their respective creators. These images are not relicensed under this HUD's GPL source license. Listing the source does not assert additional redistribution rights, ownership or endorsement by Valve or CSGOSKINS.GG.

The images are decorative substitutes for unavailable player portraits. They do not identify a player's equipped agent. A real supplied image has priority; absent or failed player images use the current side's image for observer digit 0–9. Unknown sides or unavailable/invalid observer slots retain the existing final fallback. All images load locally from the HUD package; no website or CDN is contacted by the running HUD.
